The CORSAIR HS80 RGB WIRELESS is a wireless headset that connects through its own USB receiver. Corsair's product page lists it for PC, Mac and PlayStation 4/5, with up to 20 hours of battery and a flip-up microphone. This checklist covers the Carbon colour, SKU CA-9011235-NA. The same page also offers a White version.

We have not used this headset. The facts below come from Corsair's official page for this SKU, read on 19 October 2026. Corsair also sells HS80 MAX and HS80 USB models, so the first job is to make sure you are buying the model you mean.

## Will it work with your console or computer?

Corsair's spec table names PC, Mac and PlayStation 4/5 as supported platforms. The PlayStation connection uses the included wireless USB receiver. There is also a wired USB connection, and Corsair says USB wired audio runs at 24-bit 96 kHz while wireless runs at 24-bit 48 kHz.

Xbox is not on that list. If you play on Xbox Series X|S, this is not the headset to choose. Nintendo Switch is not listed either, so treat it as unsupported unless Corsair says otherwise.

On PC, Corsair's iCUE software adds equaliser settings, pre-tuned audio profiles, sidetone control and RGB lighting. Corsair says one SLIPSTREAM receiver can connect up to three SLIPSTREAM wireless devices, which matters only if you already own other Corsair wireless gear.

## Does Dolby Atmos matter to you, and on which platform?

The page markets spatial audio with Dolby Atmos, but Corsair also states that Dolby Atmos is only available on PC. On PS5 or Mac you still get the headset's stereo drivers and wireless audio, just not Corsair's Atmos feature.

If 3D audio was the main reason you were looking at this model and you play on PlayStation, keep that limit in mind. PlayStation has its own Tempest 3D audio, but that is a console feature, not part of this headset's Atmos support.

## What do the audio and comfort specs actually say?

Corsair lists 50 mm neodymium drivers with a frequency response of 20 Hz to 40 kHz, and a headphone sensitivity of 116 dB (±3 dB). These numbers describe the hardware. They do not tell you how it will sound to you. Use them to compare with other headsets, not to predict a listening experience.

For comfort, Corsair describes memory foam ear pads covered in breathable cloth and a floating headband design. Headband pressure and ear-cup size are personal. If you wear glasses or have a larger head, check the return policy before buying.

## Is the fixed microphone a problem?

The microphone is omni-directional and, according to the spec table, not detachable. It flips up to mute and has an LED mute indicator. Corsair lists its frequency response as 100 Hz to 10 kHz.

A fixed mic is fine if you mostly play with voice chat. If you want to use the headset for music on the move without a boom mic in front of you, a model with a detachable mic will suit you better.

## How long does the battery last, and how far can you move?

Corsair rates battery life at up to 20 hours and wireless range at up to 60 feet. "Up to" means best case. Lighting effects and volume levels affect real battery life, so plan to charge it daily if you play long sessions.

## What is in the box?

According to Corsair's package contents, you get:

- The HS80 RGB WIRELESS headset
- The USB wireless transmitter
- A USB charging cable, 1.8 m
- A warranty card

There is no 3.5 mm cable listed, so this is not a headset you can plug into a controller jack.

## Before you check out

- Confirm the listing says **HS80 RGB WIRELESS**, not HS80 MAX or HS80 USB.
- Confirm the colour is **Carbon** and, where shown, the SKU **CA-9011235-NA**.
- Check your platform is PC, Mac or PS5/PS4.
- Decide whether PC-only Dolby Atmos matters to you.
- Read the seller's return terms. Corsair's own store lists its own return window; other retailers set theirs.

## When a different headset is the better buy

If you play on Xbox, need a detachable mic, or want to plug into a controller with a 3.5 mm cable, choose another model. Our [headset connection guide](/guides/headset-connection-guide/) explains how to match a headset's connection type to your platform before you buy.
