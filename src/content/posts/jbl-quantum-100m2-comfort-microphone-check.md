## Why do overseas reviewers keep returning to comfort?
Both PC-WELT and PSX Brasil make comfort a central part of their assessment, although that does not establish how this headset will fit every head. PC-WELT discusses the lightweight construction and breathable fabric padding. PSX Brasil similarly describes the plastic construction as simple, then explains why the low weight made the headset comfortable during its review.

JBL's official product information provides a narrower factual basis: fabric-covered memory-foam ear cushions, a lightweight headband, and a listed weight of 220 g. A weight specification cannot predict clamping force on a particular person, the seal around glasses, or the temperature of the room where the headset will be used.

Treat the comfort discussion as a reason to check fit rather than a promise of painless all-day use. ## What is the actual microphone disagreement?
PC-WELT describes the detachable microphone as adequate for ordinary team communication, whereas PSX Brasil is more critical of voice quality. That difference is useful because a single positive description could otherwise make a buyer assume that a removable boom guarantees a particular recording standard.

The official JBL specification confirms the detachable boom and mute feature; it does not make our page an independent microphone measurement. We have not recorded a sample with this headset. Neither publication's experience should be converted into a claim that every laptop, controller or sound interface will produce the same result.

For occasional chat, the practical question is whether the detachable arrangement and local mute control suit your routine. If your priority is a separate recording chain for broadcasts or voice production, compare that workflow independently. The headset's convenient controls do not replace an assessment of the microphone sound you need.

## Will its wired connection suit your desk?
Check the distance to the actual audio socket before choosing the 100M2: JBL lists a 1.2 m cable, and PC-WELT specifically identifies cable reach as a limitation. A laptop beside you and a desktop tower under a desk create different cable routes even when both are called PC setups.

JBL describes a 3.5 mm connection. Inspect the computer or controller documentation to see whether its socket carries both headphone audio and microphone input. PC-WELT notes that a separate headphone-and-microphone desktop arrangement can require a splitter and that its reviewed package did not include one. Do not assume that an accessory visible in another model's listing is included here.

Our [headset connection guide](/guides/headset-connection-guide/) separates the connection types before you order. This article does not promise support for every console setting or adapter. If a device has no suitable analog socket, decide on the complete connection path before expecting a passive plug to solve it.
