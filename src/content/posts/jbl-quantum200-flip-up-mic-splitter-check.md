The useful question in the overseas coverage is not whether a microphone exists, but whether its fixed flip-up design suits the way you move between team chat and ordinary listening. ## Does flipping the microphone up solve the same problem as removing it?
No: raising the Quantum 200 boom gives you its mute arrangement, while removing a detachable microphone changes what remains attached to the headset. JBL's official specification identifies a voice-focus directional flip-up boom with automatic on/off and mute features. It does not describe the boom as a detachable part.

Major HiFi treats the flip-up movement as a convenient way to stop having the microphone in front of the face when voice chat is not needed. Maxi-Geek agrees that the movement is useful, but identifies the inability to detach or retract the microphone as a drawback for listening away from a gaming setup. These are two reviewers' preferences, not a measurement of how everyone will use the headset.

That distinction changes the purchase question. If you regularly alternate between speaking and listening at the same desk, the boom can stay with the headset. If you want a visibly microphone-free pair for a commute, lifting the boom is not equivalent to taking it off. Decide which action you actually need rather than choosing from the word mute alone.

## What does the included PC splitter change?
The splitter changes the analog connector route; it does not turn this wired headset into a USB device. JBL lists the headset, a PC splitter and a foam windshield among the included items. The official cable specification separates a 1.2 m headset lead from a 1.5 m PC splitter.

Maxi-Geek would have preferred a USB option and describes the splitter as a specific desktop solution. Major HiFi also identifies a PC splitter in the package. Their agreement on its presence does not mean that the splitter is necessary for every socket. Check whether your computer uses a combined headset jack or separate headphone and microphone connectors before laying out the cable.

Do not treat the two cable lengths as one universal reach specification for every connection. A combined socket may use a different route from a computer with two separate sockets. Measure the distance to the socket you will actually use, including the part around the edge of the desk, rather than the straight distance to the monitor.

The [headset connection guide](/guides/headset-connection-guide/) explains why the connector path should be resolved before ordering. This article does not establish compatibility for every controller, adapter or operating-system setting. If a device has no suitable analog input, decide on a complete supported connection rather than assuming that any small adapter will solve it.

They point to different priorities rather than one guaranteed result. Maxi-Geek is enthusiastic about the audio presentation and describes the microphone as serviceable. Major HiFi also discusses an energetic presentation but calls out limited separation when several sound elements compete in a scene. That limitation is more useful to a reader than repeating either outlet's strongest praise without its context.

The Major HiFi review states that its listening was performed on a PlayStation 4 stereo setup. Its observations belong to that setup, game selection and reviewer. They should not be converted into a promise of a particular surround effect, competitive advantage or result on another system. We have not repeated either publication's listening tests.

## Who should choose this arrangement, and who should skip it?
Consider the Quantum 200 if you want a wired analog headset, prefer a boom that stays attached, and can match the supplied splitter to your desktop sockets. The practical attraction is a consistent chat-to-listening routine without removing and storing a separate microphone. It is not a reason to assume better voice recording or universal fit.

Skip it if you specifically need a removable boom, a USB-only connection, or wireless movement. Also compare another arrangement if a long cable route or a microphone-free appearance is central to your daily use.
