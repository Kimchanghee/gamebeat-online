GameBeat is an independent, English-language website that writes **buying guides for gaming equipment**: headsets, mice, keyboards, monitors, chairs and capture devices. It is not a store, it does not sell anything, and it is not affiliated with Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo, Valve or Amazon.

## Why does this site exist?

Equipment listings can obscure the details that decide whether a setup works: microphone routing, button reach, missing keyboard keys, monitor input modes and chair adjustment ranges. We connect manufacturer documentation with practical questions to ask before buying. These are research-based guides, not laboratory reviews.

## What will you find here?

- **Buying checklists** (the [guides](/guides/)): evergreen explainers with a setup checklist of things to confirm, each linked to the official sources it relies on and dated with the day the facts were last checked.
- **Product picks** (the [picks](/picks/)): single-product posts that point to one exact listing, once the link has been opened and matched to the exact model and option.

## Editorial standards

1. **Facts come from official sources.** Specifications, compatibility rules and connection requirements are taken from the platform holder's or manufacturer's own pages, linked at the bottom of every article.
2. **No invented experience.** We do not claim to have tested, owned or used a product unless that is literally true, and we do not publish ratings, scores or reviews we did not produce.
3. **No prices.** Prices and stock change constantly. We never print Amazon prices, availability or star ratings; you see them on the retailer page.
4. **Dates are visible.** Every page shows when it was last updated and when its facts were last checked.
5. **Corrections are welcome.** If something is wrong, email [contact@gamebeat.online](mailto:contact@gamebeat.online) and we fix it and change the updated date.
